Non-Local Effects

A wind or solar farm's shift factor tells you how strongly it's electrically coupled to a congested line, and which direction — not whether that's good or bad for that line. This tool checks each line's own binding-hour flow direction and reclassifies every farm as either worsening it (curtailing that farm would relieve the line) or relieving it (curtailing that farm would make the line worse), then lets you click through all six of the network's binding lines to see who's who.
